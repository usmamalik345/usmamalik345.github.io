About

Agents that survive production, and the full stack around them

I am a full stack developer who has built web products since 2022 with Angular, React, Node.js and Python. Since 2024 my focus has been AI agents, including voice and RAG, and the security, cost and infrastructure around them. I run apps on AWS and ship them with Docker and CI/CD.

My strongest work is where an agent meets the real world: what happens when a tool call hangs, a prompt carries an attack, a bill spikes or a database goes down. I measure those cases, fix what breaks and publish the numbers, including the weak ones. I have 3+ years of experience, including .NET production experience on a POS backend. I am based in PKT (UTC+5) and work remotely.

Work

Selected work

BleedStop

A control plane that sits in front of LLM, SMS and payment APIs. Apps ask before they spend, and get back ALLOW, LIMIT, CHALLENGE or DENY from deterministic rules. 5,433 decisions per second, p99 36.2 ms. 97.6% fewer output tokens on a capped request (64 instead of 2,618). 0 of 287,403 requests failed during a 6 pod Kubernetes rolling restart. Packaged as a Docker Compose stack, with a Helm chart for Kubernetes.
