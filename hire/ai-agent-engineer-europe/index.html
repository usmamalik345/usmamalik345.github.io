Hours

Most of the European afternoon overlaps with PKT

I work in Pakistan Time, PKT, which is UTC+5 and does not use daylight saving. Most of the EU is on Central European Time. That puts me four hours ahead in winter (CET, UTC+1) and three hours ahead in summer (CEST, UTC+2).

A 9:00 start in Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris or Warsaw is 13:00 PKT in winter and 12:00 PKT in summer. A 17:00 close there is 21:00 or 20:00 for me, which is late if we make it a daily meeting, so I would keep the late European afternoon in writing. The useful live window is the European morning and early afternoon, which is the middle of my day: standups, pairing, and reviews can happen without an async-only plan.

Practice

GDPR and the EU AI Act, as a practice, not a claim

I am not a certified GDPR practitioner, I am not registered with a European data protection authority, and I am not your data protection officer. I also do not claim EU AI Act compliance, a conformity assessment, or a risk classification for your product. Those decisions belong to your counsel. What I will do is handle data carefully and make an agent's behaviour easy to inspect.

I work on your systems under your access rules. If the agent must sit in an EU cloud region, I can build and deploy there on AWS, so personal data does not have to be copied to a machine in Pakistan for me to do the job. If production data is personal data, I would rather use a redacted, synthetic or otherwise agreed set for development than keep a local copy I do not need. I can sign an NDA.

The AI Act is about what a system does, how much a human remains in the loop, and whether you can show that later. I can document the tools an agent is allowed to call, the cases that stay on fixed rules instead of a model, the evals that have to pass before a change ships, and an audit trail of decisions. That is why BleedStop puts a decision in front of spend, with an append-only Postgres log, and why the Agent Receptionist handles emergencies and opening hours with rules at zero tokens. Those are engineering controls. They are not a legal certificate, and I will say so if you ask.
