Hours

What an Australian working day looks like from Islamabad

I work in Pakistan Time, PKT, which is UTC+5 and does not use daylight saving. Australia is ahead of me, and the useful overlap is your afternoon and my morning. I would rather say that clearly than hide it in a slogan.

In Sydney, Melbourne and Canberra, a 9:00 morning is 4:00 PKT in winter (AEST, UTC+10) and 3:00 PKT in summer (AEDT, UTC+11). That is too early for a daily standup. The shared window opens later: 9:00 PKT is 14:00 AEST or 15:00 AEDT, which is already early afternoon on the east coast. I can take a planned call in that window, during my ordinary day.

Perth is closer. 9:00 AWST (UTC+8) is 6:00 PKT, and 9:00 PKT is 12:00 AWST. That is still an early start if you want a 9:00 Perth standup every day. Midday in Perth falls inside my morning, which is a better design than asking either of us to live on the other's first hour.

Queensland stays on AEST all year, so a 9:00 Brisbane morning is 4:00 PKT in every season. Adelaide is a half hour behind the east coast: 9:00 ACST is 4:30 PKT, and 9:00 ACDT is 3:30 PKT.
