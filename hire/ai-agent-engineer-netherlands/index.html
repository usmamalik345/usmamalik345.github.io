Hours and language

A shared afternoon, and English as the working language

I work in Pakistan Time, PKT (UTC+5). The Netherlands is on Central European Time, so a 9:00 start in Amsterdam, Rotterdam or Eindhoven is 13:00 PKT in winter (CET, UTC+1) and 12:00 PKT in summer (CEST, UTC+2). That is the same overlap as the Europe hub: most of the European afternoon is inside my working day, and we can meet live in the morning your time.

How I work

Direct, written, and easy to review

If we already share a language and a long afternoon, the remaining risk is vague progress. I would rather send you something you can open than a status sentence.

I write tickets with context, open pull requests that explain what changed and how I tested it, and keep a short eval report next to the work. When we use the live CET morning, I would rather spend it on a failing case or a design choice than on recapping an email. The BleedStop case study and the Agent Receptionist case study are already written in that style, with the weak numbers left in.

Data handling follows the same practice as on the Europe page: I can deploy in the EU region you already use, I would rather develop against redacted or synthetic data, and I do not claim GDPR or EU AI Act certification. I can sign an NDA. I am open to a contract and to a full time remote role. Pricing: contact for quote.

Proof

Work you can check

I will not invent Dutch or Benelux clients, testimonials or a published rate. Here is the work that already exists.

  • BleedStop is a cost and security guard in front of LLM, SMS and payment APIs. On one shared 8 vCPU Linux machine I measured 5,433 decisions per second at p99 36.2 ms, 97.6% fewer output tokens on a capped request (64 instead of 2,618), and 0 failed requests out of 287,403 during a 6-pod Kubernetes rolling restart.
