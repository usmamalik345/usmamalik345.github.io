About

Agents that survive production, and the full stack around them

I am a full stack developer who has built web products since 2022 with Angular, React, Node.js and Python. Since 2024 my focus has been AI agents, including voice and RAG, and the security, cost and infrastructure around them. I run apps on AWS and ship them with Docker and CI/CD.

My strongest work is where an agent meets the real world: what happens when a tool call hangs, a prompt carries an attack, a bill spikes or a database goes down. I measure those cases, fix what breaks and publish the numbers, including the weak ones. I am based in PKT (UTC+5) and work remotely.
