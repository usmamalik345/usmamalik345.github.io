Hours

A shared working day with the UK

I work in Pakistan Time, PKT, which is UTC+5 and does not use daylight saving. Unlike a US East Coast morning, which lands at the end of my day, a UK morning lands in the middle of it.

A 9:00 start in the UK is 13:00 PKT in summer (BST, UTC+1) and 14:00 PKT in winter (GMT, UTC+0). A 10:00 standup in London is 14:00 or 15:00 for me, so we can pair on a failing eval or review a pull request live. A 16:00 UK review is 20:00 or 21:00 PKT, so I would keep the later UK afternoon in writing.

Data

GDPR-aware data handling, as a practice

I am not a certified GDPR practitioner, I am not registered with the ICO, and I am not your data protection officer. I will not sell a certificate I do not hold. What I will do is handle data carefully inside the controls you already have.

I work on your systems under your access rules. If the agent must sit in a UK or EU cloud region, I can build and deploy there on AWS, so the work does not have to be copied to a machine in Pakistan. If production data is personal data, I would rather use a redacted, synthetic or otherwise agreed set for development than keep a local copy I do not need.

When an agent can call a tool or spend money, the risk is also a prompt that tries to pull data out. That is why BleedStop puts a decision in front of the spend, with an append-only audit log. The defence layers are in the prompt injection post. Those are engineering controls, not a GDPR certificate. I can sign an NDA. I do not give legal advice on lawful bases, DPIAs or international transfers; your UK counsel should own those questions.
