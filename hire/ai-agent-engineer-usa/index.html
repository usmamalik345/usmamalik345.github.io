Hours

What a US working day looks like from Islamabad

I work in Pakistan Time, PKT, which is UTC+5 and does not use daylight saving. The overlap with the United States is real, but it is not a full shared morning, and I would rather say that clearly than hide it in a slogan.

A 9:00 start on the US East Coast is 18:00 PKT in summer (EDT, UTC-4) and 19:00 PKT in winter (EST, UTC-5). That is the end of my ordinary working day: enough for a standup, a planning call, or a demo. I can stay later for a planned session, but I will not treat an unplanned 11:00 Eastern call as a daily habit.

Pacific hours are harder. 9:00 PDT is 21:00 PKT, and 9:00 PST is 22:00 PKT. For a West Coast team, most of the work should be async. I can take an occasional late evening call for a 9:00 Pacific hour, but a daily Pacific standup would be a poor design for both of us.
