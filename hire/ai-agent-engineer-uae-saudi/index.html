Hours

Almost a full shared day with the Gulf

I work in Pakistan Time, PKT, which is UTC+5 and does not use daylight saving. The UAE is on Gulf Standard Time, UTC+4, all year. Saudi Arabia is on UTC+3, all year. That puts me one hour ahead of Dubai or Abu Dhabi, and two hours ahead of Riyadh or Jeddah.

A 9:00 start in the UAE is 10:00 PKT. A 9:00 start in Saudi Arabia is 11:00 PKT. A 17:00 close in Dubai is 18:00 for me, and a 17:00 close in Riyadh is 19:00 for me, so almost the whole Gulf business day sits inside an ordinary working day in Islamabad. That is a different problem from a US engagement, where Eastern hours only overlap the end of my day, and tighter still than the already large overlap I have with the UK.

Language

English, and multilingual LLM work, honestly

I work in English. I do not claim to have shipped a production Arabic chatbot or voice agent, and I am not a native Arabic copywriter. If your customers will use another language, I will say what I can build and what still has to come from you.

Modern LLM stacks can answer in more than one language when the retrieval corpus, the instructions, and the eval set are in those languages. I can build that pipeline: the agent, the retrieval, the tool calls, the cost and security guard, and the tests that fail when the model slips. What I cannot honestly offer is native Arabic marketing copy, or a claim that a model will be fluent just because it is told to be. If you need Arabic or bilingual agents, you should supply the language content and example conversations I can score, and we should measure those cases before launch.

The same rule applies to voice. I build voice agents on Pipecat and LiveKit, and I have a merged timeout fix in Pipecat. A voice agent in a second language still needs eval audio and transcripts in that language. I would rather design for that up front than promise a bilingual voice product I have not shipped.
